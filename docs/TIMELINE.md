## Rhythm

- Daily scrum: 10 minutes, same time each day, notes in `docs/scrum/daily-log.md`.
- Joint check with the other team: at least twice a week (Tue and Thu), plus a shared chat for blockers.
- A task that nobody has started 48 hours after being assigned is picked up by the core builders (see [TEAM.md](TEAM.md)).
